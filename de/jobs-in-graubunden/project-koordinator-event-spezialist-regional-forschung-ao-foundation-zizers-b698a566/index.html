Project Coordinator Event Specialist \& Regional Research (ID2228\)

The AO is a medically guided, not\-for\-profit organization, a global network of surgeons, and the world's leading education, innovation, and research organization specializing in the surgical treatment of trauma and musculoskeletal disorders. We are home to people from all over the world, from different backgrounds, with diverse talents and specialist areas. What binds us together is our passion for excellence, our dedication to our mission of improving patient care, and our understanding that we are stronger together: we are one AO.

For more information, visit

The Project Coordinator Event Specialist \& Regional Research coordinates and supports the implementation of AO Foundation's research activities within the region, connecting global research and development priorities with regional research communities and initiatives. The role supports research grants, fellowships, research officers and committees, research education and regional research projects, while facilitating collaboration among researchers, AO governance bodies and relevant internal and external stakeholders. Through effective coordination, project management and stakeholder engagement, the role strengthens regional research capabilities, collaboration and the visibility of AO research.

This is a temporary role until end of 2027, with the possibility of extension.

Main Responsibilities:

Research Coordinator

  • Coordinate and implement regional research activities across all clinical specialties, including research fellowships, grants, and awards.
  • Manage internal and external communication related to regional research projects and initiatives.
  • Prepare and coordinate contracts and process invoices related to research fellowships and grants.
  • Monitor and manage regional research budgets in close collaboration with the Regional Managers.
  • Ensure accurate documentation, storage, and archiving of regional research records and related project documentation.

Event Specialist

  • Support the planning, coordination, and delivery of assigned educational events within the DACH cluster.
  • Provide operational and administrative support throughout the event lifecycle and maintain accurate and up\-to\-date information in the event management system.
  • Manage participant\-related activities for assigned educational events, including registration and participant communication.
  • Provide on\-site event support and act as a backup to Event Owners when required.
  • Facilitate effective collaboration and coordination within operational teams and with relevant stakeholders.
  • Coordinate and manage collaboration with local agencies and external suppliers.
  • Process invoices and support budget monitoring and P\&L reporting for assigned events.
  • Coordinate CME accreditation, compliance approvals, and the maintenance of relevant data in Concert.

Main Requirements:

  • Bachelor degree in relevant field (e.g., hospitality, event management, public relations, communications, marketing) or higher Education "Fachausweis"
  • Proven record of professional experience in event organization or project management (3\-5 years)
  • Ideally experience in the Medical Education field
  • Strong organizational, project and event management skills
  • communication skills and ability to build and maintain relations with Health Care Professional at all levels and broader stakeholders
  • problem solving and strong analytical skills as well as proven ability to prioritize task
  • Fluency in English and (written and spoken)
  • Travel: willingness to travel up to 20% (mainly regional travel within DACH region and once or twice a year intrenationally)
  • Office presence: 2x/week in Zizers office (possibility to work from Davos HQ)
  • An interesting and varied job in an exciting and innovative organization
  • The opportunity to be part of a highly committed international team
  • Modern infrastructure
  • High degree of flexibility regarding working hours and location (depending on operational requirements)
  • Generous pagacke of social benefits, including supplementary vacation days and pension scheme contributions
  • Internal skills training opportunities and support for continued education
