Vollständige Beschreibung

Are you ready to use innovation to improve the lives of millions of people? Do you think one step ahead, see opportunities where others see limits, and want to invest your energy in something meaningful? If so, INTEGRA is the right place for you. Our solutions are not created by chance – they are the result of passion, precision and team spirit. With every technology we develop, we help people around the world to research diseases, perform diagnoses and enable new therapies. Whether in diagnostics, research or quality control, our instruments – made in Switzerland and the USA – make laboratory work safer, more efficient and more sustainable.

With us, you can make changes that will shape the world. Does this sound like what you're looking for? Then join our team and help to accelerate scientific discovery. What you can expect from the role This is a new role, and you get to shape it. As our Demand Generation Marketing Manager, you help decide which of our established products get marketing focus each year and build the customer journeys that turn interest into sales-ready leads – worldwide, including the US.

  • Help select the focus products each year and set campaign goals, segments and key messages
  • Build customer journeys, from lead capture and nurturing to scoring and sales handover
  • Own paid search on Google Ads and Microsoft Ads for all products, working with our SEA agency
  • Plan, run and optimise social media advertising on LinkedIn, Meta and other platforms
  • Manage our media channels and the agency behind them
  • Track campaigns from first touch to opportunity and move budget to what works
  • Brief creative teams and agencies, and align with US Commercial Marketing and the regions

What you offer

  • Degree in marketing, business, communications or similar, or equivalent experience
  • 5+ years in B2B demand generation or performance marketing, 2+ of them owning programmes end to end
  • Experience in life sciences, lab products or another technical B2B market is highly preferred
  • Experience turning product positioning into campaign messages
  • Hands-on experience with Google Ads, Microsoft Ads and paid social, including steering agencies
  • Lead nurturing and scoring in Salesforce Account Engagement or a similar platform
  • Strong analytical skills: funnels, Google Analytics, conversion tracking and attribution
  • Organised and confident with budgets, agencies and several campaigns at once
  • Fluent English
  • German is a plus
