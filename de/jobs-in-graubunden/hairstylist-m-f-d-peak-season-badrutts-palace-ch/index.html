Your key responsibilities

As part of our Palace Coiffeur Team, you will contribute to delivering an exceptional and highly personalised experience to our international guests. Your key responsibilities will include:

  • Providing professional, personalised consultations and hair services tailored to our guests’ individual needs and preferences

  • Performing colouring services with confidence, precision and a high level of technical expertise

  • Recommending and selling selected premium wellness, beauty and haircare products

  • Ensuring consistently high standards of service, presentation and guest care in line with the standards of a five-star luxury hotel


• What you bring to the job

For this varied and guest-focused position, you ideally bring:

  • Several years of professional experience as a Hair Stylist / Hairdresser, preferably within a luxury hospitality, high-end salon or international environment

  • Strong expertise in professional haircare and colouring techniques

  • Very good command of English; German, Italian and any additional languages are considered an advantage

  • A natural ability to understand and anticipate guests’ individual needs

  • A strong service orientation and passion for creating memorable guest experiences

  • Excellent teamwork and interpersonal skills

  • Flexibility, resilience and a high level of commitment

  • A polished and professional appearance, combined with excellent manners and discretion


Our offer

  • A fixed-term seasonal position from 18.12.2026 to 03.01.2027

  • An average 5-day working week of 43.5 hours

  • 5 weeks of annual leave and 6 public holidays per year, calculated pro rata

  • 13th-month salary from the start of employment, calculated pro rata temporis

  • Accommodation in one of our attractive staff residences, subject to availability and upon request

  • Meals available at our modern staff restaurant

  • The opportunity to work in an exceptional international luxury hospitality environment

  • A dynamic workplace offering professional development opportunities


If you are looking for a rewarding, challenging and varied position in a dynamic luxury environment and would like to become part of a dedicated and well-established team, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
